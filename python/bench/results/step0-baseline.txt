# Renderer core, step 0: counters and baseline, on a quiet machine (2026-11-16)

Measured in a quiet window (no other heavy job on the machine, every heavy slot
held for it), 23:53 to 00:07, in 13 minutes of an 18-minute limit. The 1-minute load average was 1.0 to
4.0 across the window, and is beside every row in the table files. The run under load that this
replaces is step0-baseline-loaded.txt, with its files named "-step0-loaded".

Machine: an Apple Silicon Mac (M-series Max, 18 cores, 64 GB), macOS 27, on mains power.
Build: Release, preset release-notorch (CALIPER_WITH_TORCH=OFF), Python 3.13, torch 2.15.0 (MPS).
Commits: after = 3717239 (step 0: counters, Metal statistics, points row; the later step-0 commits change
no binary); before = bae8c25 (its parent on sprint4-build). Viewer: caliper-viewer --headless --stats (an
offscreen canvas that polls and draws about 50 times a second; no window may open on this machine).

What ran, interleaved, in this order: three rounds of (1024² before, 1024² after, 1,000,000 points), then
the graphics suite's geometry timing row, then one repeat each of 256², 2048², 100,000 and 4,000,000
points, then bench_show.py window and attach at 1024² with 5 s rows. Every bench_live.py table ran the
modes record60, sampler, record and nosampler (the plain baseline mode was left out to fit the window;
the thresholds compare against the commit-matched controls). Files: S-table-mps1024-step0-r1..r3.txt
(after), S-table-mps1024-step0-before-r1..r3.txt (before), S-table-points-mps1000000-step0-r1..r3.txt,
S-table-mps256-step0.txt, S-table-mps2048-step0.txt, S-table-points-mps100000-step0.txt,
S-table-points-mps4000000-step0.txt, P-window-mps-1024-step0.txt, P-attach-mps-1024-step0.txt.

The spread of steps per second between the three repeats is 0.2 to 3.0 points of the control (sampler
and nosampler: 1024² after 3.0 and 0.2, before 1.0 and 2.1; 1,000,000 points 2.3 and 0.7). Three of the
six spreads exceed the 2-point threshold, by up to 1 point, so a step's comparison needs at least three
interleaved repeats of each build and should judge medians.
Before against after at 1024²: steps per second as a share of the control differ by 0.6 points (sampler)
and 0.5 points (nosampler), within the threshold; the nosampler caliper.show median differs by 0.6 µs and
the latency by 0.03 ms (median) and 0.14 ms (p95), within theirs. The sampler caliper.show median reads
40.0 µs before and 55.9 µs after, but its repeats overlap (36.8 to 52.2 µs before, 37.6 to 56.0 µs after)
and both rose with the load across the window (1.0 to 3.7); step 0 changed no code the producer runs
(python/src/_live.mm, src/host/live/live_client.mm and the Python package are unchanged), so it is noise
of the 60 Hz sampler's few hundred samples, and the next step should take more repeats of that row.
