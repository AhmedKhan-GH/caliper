# Roadmap

<div id="roadmap-graph" class="roadmap-window" data-graph="graph.json"></div>

The roadmap shows every feature of Caliper, shipped, built or planned, as one graph per pillar that reads from left to right, from what a feature needs to what it unblocks. On a wide screen, hover a feature for its summary, click it to open its page, and drag or scroll to move. On a phone, the list of pillars below takes the graph's place. The registry behind the graph is the set of YAML files in the repository's [`roadmap/`](https://github.com/AhmedKhan-GH/caliper/tree/main/roadmap) folder.

## Pillars

- [Core runtime and SDK](core.md)
- [Rendering and 3-D](rendering.md)
- [Live data, streaming and recordings](streaming.md)
- [Builder and specs](builder.md)
- [Agents and automation](agents.md)
- [Runs and comparison](runs.md)
- [Interpretability and interventions](interpretability.md)
- [Web and embedding](embedding.md)
- [Platforms and distribution](platforms.md)

[Candidates](candidates/index.md) lists ideas that are not yet in the registry, grouped by pillar, including seven proposed pillars.
